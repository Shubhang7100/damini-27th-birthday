A note from the heart

Dear Damini,

Happy birthday, you wonderful human.

I hope 27 is kinder to you. I hope it brings you the kind of mornings that feel light, the kind of evenings that let you exhale, and the kind of surprises that remind you life can be beautiful without you having to work so hard for every little thing.

I admire how you keep moving towards the life you imagine, even when the present asks a lot of you. You show up on the difficult days. You keep learning, building, trying, and believing. And while that warrior in you is something to celebrate, I hope this year also gives you permission to put the armour down sometimes.

You deserve more than milestones and accomplishments. You deserve peace that stays, people who make things easier, laughter that catches you off guard, love that feels safe, and moments where you don't have to prove a single thing to anyone.

May the year ahead surprise you in the loveliest ways. May your hard work find its way back to you as opportunities, your patience as progress, and your dreams as memories you get to live.

And please, for the sake of this website's future photo gallery, take more pictures. A birthday tribute should not have to operate on a three-photo budget. I am doing my best here.

Here's to you, Damini. Always keep shining. ✨

“May 27 be the year you realise that you deserve the same kindness you keep giving the world.”

One last thing

A tiny birthday secret

If the world gets loud, I hope you never forget this: you are already enough, even on the days when you feel behind. You don't have to earn rest, affection, or a little magic. Today, let the celebration find you. Happy 27th, Damini. ♡
